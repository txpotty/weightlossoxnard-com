    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Oxnard, CA

    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Oxnard

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Because it hits both, it lowers DHT in the blood by more than 90 percent, compared with roughly 70 percent for finasteride, which blocks only type 2. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil adds a separate benefit by lengthening the growth phase. Together they handle both sides.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride. A year of steady finasteride with photos is the usual bar before stepping up. Some men with aggressive early loss discuss it sooner. It is not used in women who could become pregnant.

    Dosing and Schedule in Oxnard

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Its long half-life lets some providers use fewer doses per week. Minoxidil is applied daily or taken orally. Oral minoxidil is off-label.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. That early shed is common, and it typically settles by month three as follicles move into a new growth phase. By months three to six, many men notice less loss and some thickening. Comparison trials favored dutasteride over finasteride for hair counts. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Dutasteride + Minoxidil in Oxnard

    Dutasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in a small share of men, similar to finasteride. The long half-life means side effects can take weeks to months to clear. Report low mood promptly. Scalp itch is the usual topical issue. Oral minoxidil may cause body hair or swelling.
